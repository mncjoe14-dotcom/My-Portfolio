About me

A developer who cares about the last 5% of polish.

I've spent the past several years building websites and web apps for small studios, founders, and agencies — mostly the kind of work where the interface has to hold up on a phone in bright sunlight just as well as on a widescreen monitor.

I like projects with a clear structure and a strong point of view: fast-loading pages, layouts that don't break, and details — spacing, type, motion — that feel considered rather than default. Off-screen, I'm usually near the water, which is where the palette on this page comes from.
